# Asset replacement guide

Add owner-approved files at the following paths, then rebuild. Missing logo, founder and vehicle files are checked at build time and have labeled safe fallbacks rather than broken images.

| Path | Purpose | Suggested size |
|---|---|---|
| `public/brand/trustbridge-logo.png` | Official circular emblem, transparent padding | 512 × 512 |
| `public/images/founder.jpg` | Genuine approved portrait of Ishan Wijesinghe | 800 × 1000 |
| `public/images/vehicles/axio.jpg` | Actual Toyota Axio Hybrid 2016 | 1200 × 800 |
| `public/images/vehicles/wagon-r.jpg` | Actual Suzuki Wagon R Stingray Hybrid 2018 | 1200 × 800 |
| `public/images/kandy.webp` | Licensed temple hero and destination image | 1600 × 733 |

Use genuine photographs with permission. Avoid visible private registration plates, bystander identity or sensitive information unless permission is established. Keep images compressed, generally below 300 KB. Maintain source, author, license and modifications in IMAGE-CREDITS.md and update the public credits page. Do not replace the current temple image with a different location without changing both locale captions and alt text. No stock people are presented as customers. Add future genuine customer reviews only with approval; no testimonials are currently rendered.
